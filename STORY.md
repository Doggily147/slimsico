# Yellow

*Mini introduction. Matches the opening shot in `renders/opening.mp4`.*

## The premise

Yellow falls out of a perfectly clear sky onto a world that is nothing but a
numbered grid stretching to the horizon. He does not know where he came from,
what the numbers mean, or why he is wearing a crown. Everything he learns, he
learns by walking off the edge of the tile he landed on.

## Opening

Nobody falls out of a clear blue sky. There is no cloud to fall from, no plane,
no cliff. And yet here comes a small yellow shape, arms windmilling, tumbling
end over end, getting bigger and bigger and bigger.

He lands on his belly with a slap that echoes off nothing at all.

For a while he just lies there. The ground is cool and smooth and white, and
it is covered in lines. Right in front of his nose, in careful grey and blue,
someone has written **33** and **31**.

He does not know what that means. He does not know what anything means. He is
not even sure what *he* is, except that he is yellow, and that his tummy hurts.

He pushes himself up onto his hands. Then his knees. Then, wobbling like a
newborn, onto his feet. The ground goes on in every direction, tile after tile
after tile, every one of them numbered, all the way to a horizon so flat it
looks drawn with a ruler.

Something slides forward over one eye. He reaches up and touches it. Gold.
Pointy. Heavier than it looks.

A crown.

Yellow looks at the empty sky he fell from. He looks at the numbers under his
feet. He looks at the crown in his hands.

"...Oh," he says, which is the first thing he has ever said.

He puts the crown back on, because it seems like the sort of thing you should
not leave lying around, and picks a direction.

## Where it can go

- **The grid is a map, not a floor.** Each tile number is a coordinate in
  something much bigger. Yellow landed on 33,31 for a reason. Someone, or
  something, aimed him.
- **The crown is not his.** Or it is, and he is the only one who has forgotten.
  Either version gives every character he meets a reason to react to him.
- **Other things fall too.** Objects, then creatures, then eventually a second
  person. The sky is a delivery system, and Yellow was the first parcel.
- **He is very good at getting back up.** That is his whole skill. He cannot
  fight or fly or solve puzzles, but he can be knocked flat a hundred times and
  push himself up a hundred and one. It is played for laughs until it is not.

## Tone

Warm, dry and a little melancholy. Think of a lost toy in an empty room that
decides to be cheerful about it. Physical comedy from the wobbly body, quiet
moments from the empty horizon. He never gets a big speech. He gets a small
sigh and a next step.
